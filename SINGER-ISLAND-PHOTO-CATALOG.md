# Singer Island photo catalogue

A by-eye catalogue of John Oliver's Singer Island photo library, made 2026-09-30 so that blog and condo pages can pick
verified, non-repeating photos without re-inspecting the whole folder. It covers the Singer Island articles on this site
and is meant to be reused by the condowpb.com Singer Island section.

**Source:** OneDrive, `DO Homes Group Master/Website Folders/Doyouneedphotos/Singer Island` (plus its `Other Building`
subfolder). The library is John's; nothing in it was moved, renamed or edited. Copies used on the site are listed in
`public/images/singer-island/SOURCES.md`, with crops, blurs and verification notes.

**This file is public** (the repository is public). Privacy problems are described by type only: no names, house or unit
numbers, phone numbers or OneDrive IDs are recorded here. Check the original before using any flagged frame.

## How to read it

- **VERIFIED:** something in the frame ties it to Singer Island, Palm Beach Shores, Phil Foster Park or the Lake Worth
  Inlet: the Port of Palm Beach cranes, a "Tropical" container ship, the Blue Heron Bridge, legible signage, or a
  chain of evidence (for example, other frames from the same balcony show those landmarks).
- **CONSISTENT:** looks like the island (beach, towers, lagoon) but nothing in the frame proves it. Use only with a
  caption that claims no more than the frame shows, or confirm the location with John first.
- **DOUBTFUL:** evidence points elsewhere. Do not use for Singer Island.
- **Used on the site:** `*` marks uses that are in PR #55 and not yet live.

Counts by verdict: CONSISTENT 145, DOUBTFUL 3, N/A 5, VERIFIED 75, – 1, — 1. 230 files in total.

## Findings that matter

1. **Being in the folder is not proof of location.** `IMG_0153` shows the Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse. `IMG_5564` looks
   like a storm over the mainland shore.
2. **The inlet sunset evening (`IMG_2939`–`IMG_2980`) is the Lake Worth Inlet**, shot from the south tip of Singer
   Island with Palm Beach on the far shore (confirmed by John 2026-09-30). About 17 frames show the port cranes, and
   `IMG_2956`/`IMG_2957` show a "Tropical" container ship.
3. **The only legible "Riviera Beach Municipal Beach" sign is in frames on the owner blocklist** (identifiable people).
   A retake of that sign without people would give the beach sections a verified public-access photo.
4. **The `Other Building` subfolder** is one condo tower's pool deck, rooftop, a unit interior and views from it. Its
   rooftop views (`IMG_0521`, `IMG_0522`) show the Blue Heron Bridge, the port cranes and the West Palm Beach skyline, so
   the same tower's beach views are verified by chain of evidence. The building is not named anywhere in the frames.
5. **Coverage may be incomplete.** The folder listing stops at 200 entries. Later files were found by trying exact names
   (checked `IMG_5561`–`5600` and `IMG_5844`–`5880`). Not checked: `IMG_5601`–`5843`, anything above `5880`, other
   formats (.MOV, .HEIC, .PNG) and names that don't start with `IMG_`. A plain file list of the folder would close this.
6. **Videos** (`.MP4`) were listed but not reviewed.
