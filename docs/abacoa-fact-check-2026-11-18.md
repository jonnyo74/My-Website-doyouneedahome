# Abacoa village fact-check, applied 2026-11-18

Source: a ChatGPT research pass checked against the Abacoa POA directory, each
village's own HOA site, Florida Sunbiz, county and Town of Jupiter records and
developer sites. It was not independently re-verified here, so spot-check the
corrections below before relying on them in a listing presentation.

## Deliberately not published

HOA assessment amounts (the research found 2026 quarterly figures for Charleston
Court and Martinique), officer and director names, and registered agents. They
change, and the house rule for these pages is no fee figures.
