# Abacoa village photos needed

Today every village page uses the Abacoa Town Center sunset street as its hero,
captioned "Abacoa Town Center". None shows the village. Village pages pick up
photos automatically from each community's `photos` array in
`src/lib/communities.ts` (first photo is the hero, the rest form a gallery), so
nothing else needs to change when photos arrive.

Only use photos you shot or are cleared to use, and confirm each is the village
named. Landscape, at least 1600 px wide.
