# Palm Beach Gardens photo sources

Same format as `../jupiter/SOURCES.md`. Every frame that goes on a Palm Beach
Gardens page is listed here first: file, original, size, source, what it shows,
where it is used. Location is settled from the content of the frame, from John's
word, or from EXIF that agrees with the content. A phone GPS fix on its own is
not enough; the Jupiter library carried several stale fixes, and this one does too.

## Uncommitted master frames

`public/public/Palm Beach Gardens/` holds six JPEGs (1920 px on the long side,
no EXIF) that are not in git and not on any page: `211.JPG` (the Parker Bridge
golf frame, same as palm-beach-gardens-005), `IMG_0869.JPG` and `IMG_7081.JPG`
(a Mediterranean clock tower with a fountain wall, day and dusk), `IMG_7085.JPG`
(a "Frenchman's Reserve" sign wall), `IMG_7086.JPG` (the lit fountain, same as
pbg-003) and `IMG_9348.JPG` (the fish panels, same as pbg-005). None goes on a
page until John names the location and the frame is listed above.

## Not in this folder, and why

- The Jupiter library's lighthouse, beach and Riverwalk frames are Jupiter.
- `../port-st-lucie/local-pga-*` are PGA Golf Club in Port St. Lucie, not PGA
  National; see `../pga-national/SOURCES.md`.
- John D. MacArthur Beach State Park is at 10900 Jack Nicklaus Drive, North
  Palm Beach. Any frame of it belongs to that town's folder.
