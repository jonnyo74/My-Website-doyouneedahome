# Lotus (west Boca Raton) imagery — provenance

**Everything here is John's own photography**, shot at Lotus on the same day as the
8804 Skyward Street listing shoot (August 2026).

This folder belongs to the **`/communities/lotus` page**. The listing folder
`public/images/listings/8804-skyward-street/` holds its own processed copies of many of the
same frames. That is deliberate (John, 2026-09-23): the listing folder is for the listing and
goes away when it sells, and the community page must not depend on it. Don't "dedupe" by
pointing the page back at the listing folder.

## Originals — `_source/` (not committed)

`IMG_6280`–`IMG_6312`, 16 files, 4032×3024 and 5712×4284, ≈31 MB. John first dropped them in
`public/public/Delray Beach/Lotus/`. That was the wrong city, since Lotus is Boca, and it wasn't
git-ignored. They were moved here on 2026-09-23. `public/images/**/_source/` is in `.gitignore`,
so the full-size files stay off the site.

These are byte-identical to the listing's own `_source/` copies.
