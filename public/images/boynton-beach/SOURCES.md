Ocean Ridge**, not inside Boynton Beach city limits. Alt text and captions should call it the
Boynton Inlet and must not imply it is within the city. The identification rests on the
photographer's tag plus the scene matching the inlet; it has not been confirmed on site.

`heroImagePosition: '68% 50%'` to keep the boat in the phone crop.
