# Mirasol imagery: provenance

## 2026-09-26 drop: John's own photos

John shot these at Mirasol (Palm Beach Gardens) and dropped the raws into the master
library at `public/public/Mirasol/IMG_6902–6907.jpg` (iPhone, 5712×4284 and 4032×3024,
camera metadata stripped on export). The raws are **not committed**. The three below are
2048×1536 q80 mozjpeg copies, matching the size of the existing `mirasol-00N` files.

| File | From | Depicts | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mirasol-sign-lake-fountain.jpg` | `IMG_6906` | "Mirasol" sign wall beside a lake with a jet fountain, blue sky | Community **hero** and OG image; *Best Neighborhoods in Palm Beach Gardens* (Mirasol/BallenIsles/Old Palm section) |
| `mirasol-fountain-roundabout.jpg` | `IMG_6905` | Tiered white fountain in a roundabout, date palms, coleus beds | Community gallery (tall bento tile); *Local Guide to Palm Beach Gardens* ("The gated golf communities") |
| `mirasol-sign-lake-storm.jpg` | `IMG_6907` | Same sign wall and lake fountain, wider, under storm clouds | Community gallery |

No people, and no branding other than Mirasol's own sign.

**Captions stay subject-only.** The frames show a sign wall and a roundabout. Nothing in
them proves which gate or road they sit on, so captions don't call either one "the main
entrance."

elevation, kitchen, living room, bedroom and sitting area. They are the same frames as
`public/public/Mirasol/IMG_0514`, `IMG_0547`–`0550` and `246F3DF6-….jpg`. Provenance was
not recorded when they were added. Until 2026-09-26 they were the whole gallery, so the
hero was a single house's pool. They now sit behind the three community shots.
