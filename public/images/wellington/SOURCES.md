All eight files here are **our own photography**, not stock.

## What they actually are

`wellington-001` through `wellington-008` are all frames from a **single polo shoot** —
same venue, same match, same afternoon, all taken from the spectator side of the field.
There is no other subject in this folder.

## Open question — do not caption these with a venue name

The sideline boards read **U.S. Polo Assn.**, which points to the **National Polo Center –
Wellington** (USPA-owned, and known as the International Polo Club Palm Beach before the
2022 rebrand). That is an inference from branding, not a confirmation.

Polo is also played in the area at Grand Champions Polo Club and elsewhere, and the fields
look alike. **Ask John which shoot this was before naming the venue in a caption.**

Same discipline as the Blowing Rocks / Coral Cove question — visually indistinguishable
locations get verified, not guessed.

Until that is confirmed, captions in `src/lib/articles.ts` describe **the subject**
("a winter-season polo match in Wellington", "the grandstand side of a match") rather
than asserting a specific club.

## Still needed (local photography)

The article set covers far more than polo, and there is currently no imagery for any of it:

- **Wellington Environmental Preserve (Section 24)** — the six-story observation tower and
  the boardwalk. This is the single biggest gap; it anchors the hidden-gems article.
- **Peaceful Waters Sanctuary** boardwalk.
- **The bridle trails** — a sand trail running beside a road or canal would illustrate half
  a dozen articles.
- **Show jumping at Wellington International** — the festival is the other half of the
  village's identity and we have no frame of it.
- **The Wellington Amphitheater** on an event night.
- **Residential streetscapes** — a gated community entry, a Binks Forest oak canopy, an
  equestrian street with fence lines and paddocks.
- **The State Road 7 corridor**, including Lotis and the K-Park site, for the development
  coverage in the eating-and-drinking article.

Because every existing file is the same subject, inline photos have deliberately been
limited to the articles where polo is genuinely the topic. Adding any of the above would
let the rest of the set carry images too.
