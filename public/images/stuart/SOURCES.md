# Stuart imagery — provenance

Files prefixed `local-` are **our own photography** — not stock.

## Important: these were shot on the Jupiter stretch of the Intracoastal

They come from the `Doyouneedphotos/Jupiter Intracoastal` shoot, not from Stuart.

They are used here as **subject illustration only**, exactly the way licensed stock is
used elsewhere on the site, and their captions in `src/lib/articles.ts` deliberately
describe the *subject* — a dock, a boat lift, a moored sailboat, a residential waterway —
rather than asserting a location. No caption says or implies "this is Stuart."

Replace them with genuine Stuart photography when we have it. The St. Lucie River, the
Roosevelt Bridge and the downtown Riverwalk would all be better.

## Frames deliberately excluded

The same shoot contains a lot of unusable material:

- **Anything with the Jupiter Lighthouse in frame** (`IMG_1315`, `1316`, `1318`, `1321`,
  `1326`, `1327` and others). The lighthouse is an unmistakable location tell — using it
  on a Stuart page would be a straightforward lie about place.
- **Selfies** (`IMG_1319`–`IMG_1323`), which show an identifiable person and a branded cap.
- Frames with a thumb over the lens, and the jet-ski shots, which show a rider.

## Screening rules applied

Anything used here must be:

- **Free of identifiable people** — no likeness/release risk.
- **Free of third-party branding**, including legible vessel names and hailing ports.
- **Free of landmarks that identify a different place.**
- **Captioned by subject, never by place.**

A strong Unsplash sportfisher shot was rejected on the second rule: it carried a legible
vessel name and hailing port across the transom plus identifiable crew on deck.
