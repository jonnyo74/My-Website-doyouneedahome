## Comparison schematic for boca-raton-vs-nearby-cities (added 2026-09-17)

Original graphics, not photographs and not licensed from anyone: generated by
`scripts/generate-boca-comparison-graphic.mjs` (sharp/librsvg, Georgia and Segoe UI). No credit line needed.

- **boca-raton-nearby-cities-panel.webp** — 1200x1500 (4:5). Desktop panel of the split hero. All 13 places the
  article compares, placed north-to-south by latitude, with the Palm Beach / Broward county line.
- **boca-raton-nearby-cities-mobile.webp** — 1200x800 (3:2). Delray Beach to Fort Lauderdale; the northern places
  are named in a line of text at the top rather than drawn.
- **boca-raton-nearby-cities-social.webp** — 2400x1257. Open Graph / Twitter / BlogPosting image: title text left,
  the panel right.
  Used on (all three): `boca-raton-vs-nearby-cities`

Coordinates are the Wikipedia infobox points for each municipality (checked 2026-09-17). The coastline and
county-line positions are approximate. East–west spacing is exaggerated so the labels fit, and the graphic says so.
It is deliberately not a map: no roads, distances, scale bar or drive times. Borders the article relies on
(Highland Beach and Deerfield Beach adjoin Boca) come from the City's comprehensive plan, Intergovernmental
Coordination Element. If the comparison set changes, edit `PLACES` in the script and re-run it.
