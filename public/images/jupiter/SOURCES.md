## The older named and numbered files

`jupiter-003.jpg` through `jupiter-022.jpg` are byte-identical to files of the same name in John's curated library (Content for Site/Jupiter/Killers), so they are his. `jupiter-lighthouse-sunset.jpg` is a 1600 × 1600 copy of `jupiter-october-2019-012.jpg` from the same library, with a filter applied; it clipped the lighthouse when cropped as a wide hero, which is why the "living in" article now uses fresh crops of the full-resolution original. The other named files (`jupiter-inlet.jpg` (removed from the things-to-do article 2026-10-02; still the hidden-gems hero), `jupiter-waterfront-dining.jpg`, `jupiter-luxury-home.jpg` (removed from the neighborhoods article 2026-10-02; still the cost-of-living hero), `jupiter-pelican-dock.jpg` (removed from the neighborhoods article 2026-10-02; still the pros-and-cons hero; was also inline on the who-should-move article until its 2026-10-02 rebuild), `jupiter-waterway-dusk.jpg` (no longer the who-should-move hero as of 2026-10-02), `jupiter-oceanfront-condo.jpg`, `jupiter-jet-ski-lighthouse.jpg`, now unused, and `jupiter-inlet-palms.jpg`, both removed from the things-to-do article 2026-10-02; inlet-palms is still inline on the pros-and-cons and eat-drink articles (dropped from who-should-move in its 2026-10-02 rebuild)) have no recorded origin yet; the remaining June 2026 Jupiter articles still use them. `IMG_2233.JPG`, `IMG_2429.JPG`, `IMG_2578.JPG`, `IMG_2579.JPG`, `IMG_2589.JPG` and `IMG_2622.JPG` match John's Doyouneedphotos/Jupiter library by name and size and are his; none is used on a page yet.

The 2019 shoot's EXIF GPS repeats one coordinate across different days with a DOP of 65, so location for those frames rests on what is visible. The 2022 beach frames carry a precise GPS fix on the beach between Ocean Cay Park and Carlin Park.

`jupiter-009.jpg` carries EXIF GPS of 26°47′45″N 80°02′25″W (2021-06-02), which is the Lake Worth Lagoon near West Palm Beach, not Jupiter. It is still inline on the hidden-gems article captioned as the Jupiter inlet; that caption is not supported by the file's own data and should be replaced when that article is rebuilt. `jupiter-005.jpg` and `jupiter-008.jpg` also carry GPS fixes that do not match their content, but both show the Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse, which settles the location. `jupiter-017.jpeg` (a bronze statue on a jetty) and `jupiter-022.jpg` (a bird against clouds) carry no location evidence and are not used. `IMG_2233.JPG` shows the Square Grouper's stage (sign in frame) with performers' faces and is not used; `IMG_2429.JPG`, `IMG_2578.JPG`, `IMG_2579.JPG`, `IMG_2589.JPG` and `IMG_2622.JPG` have no EXIF and no landmark that settles the location, so they are not used.
