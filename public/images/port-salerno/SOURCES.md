`stock-manatee-underwater.jpg` is the in-body image on `manatee-pocket-guide`. Shot at Caye
Caulker, Belize, in clear water over sand. **Used at 4:3 in-body rather than as a hero** —
a 3.4:1 band cuts the snout off the bottom and the skull off the top, and the head-on
portrait is the whole point of the frame. The caption says outright that the Pocket does
not look like this, so the clarity of the water does not set an expectation the estuary
cannot meet.

`local-manatee-pocket-marina.jpg` is the hero on `manatee-pocket-guide`, and it is the only
image in this folder actually shot in Port Salerno. Band taken from `top: 640`. That makes
the water honest: the Pocket runs dark and tannic, not the turquoise of every stock
alternative screened for this article.

**It carries a rental sign with a phone number and a hull badge.** Our screening rule bars
third-party branding, but that rule exists for *stock*, where the risks are implying a
relationship or faking a location. This is our own documentary photograph of a public
marina, where incidental signage is ordinary editorial content. Judgement call, made
deliberately — do not treat it as licence to accept branding in stock.

Two more Port Salerno frames sit in the same folder: `IMG_3311.JPEG` (boatyard with travel

## Rejected — do not retry

Both of these are **Unsplash+ premium**: they require a paid subscription and are not
covered by the free Unsplash License.

- `7CD0XAin6Q4` — paddleboarder at sunset, Islamorada
- `-XqY0NrRU-Y` — row of pineapples on a wooden shelf, Elise Lainé

**Unsplash+ images are hard to spot when picking.** Search results interleave them with
free photos and the URL is identical in form, so a copied link gives no clue. On the photo
page they carry a small "Plus" badge and a lock icon by the download button. If a download
returns a ~7KB file instead of an image, it is gated — that is the tell.

## Do not use Intracoastal or luxury-home shots here

This includes the five `local-` files in `public/images/stuart/`. They depict private
docks, boat lifts and moored sailboats on the Jupiter Intracoastal — affluent residential
waterfront. Port Salerno is a working fishing village with a very different housing stock
and price tier, and those images misrepresent it. The same warning was already recorded in
a code comment on the previous Port Salerno hero.

## Archival — the best source for the history article

The **1923 Manatee Pocket photograph** is the right hero for
`history-of-port-salerno-florida`. Original typed caption reads
"MANATEE POCKET SALERNO, FLA, NEAR STUART, AND NEAR INLET." Two men on a dock, a launch
with a canoe on the cabin roof, houseboats across the water.

- Item: https://martindigitalhistory.org/items/show/5013
- Identifier: `Thurlow-2024-022-SCC-044`
- Creator: Florida Photographic Concern (Fort Pierce)
- From the *Stuart Commercial Club Deep Water Harbor Proposal, 1923* collection,
  Martin County Library System
- Rights: item page states public domain in the United States. Independently sound —
  US works published through 1930 are out of copyright.

**Why it is not in this folder yet:** the freely downloadable web derivative carries a
visible "Martin Digital History" watermark in the bottom-right, which fails our
no-third-party-branding screening rule. **The archivist offers higher-resolution files on
request** — https://www.martindigitalhistory.org/contact. Request the unwatermarked scan,
then set `heroImage` on the history article.

Credit line to use when it lands:

> Manatee Pocket, Salerno, 1923. Florida Photographic Concern. *Stuart Commercial Club
> Deep Water Harbor Proposal, 1923* collection, Martin County Library System
> (Thurlow-2024-022-SCC-044).

## Check Florida Memory first for anything else

https://www.floridamemory.com/ — State Archives of Florida. 200,000+ images under
**Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0**: free, commercial use fine, credit requested
under s. 257.35(6), Fla. Stat. No permissions chase and no fee question, so anything
available there beats the alternatives. Search "Port Salerno", "Salerno", "Manatee Pocket".

Credit as: `Courtesy of the State Archives of Florida, Florida Memory. [Item title and number.]`

## Screening rules

- Free of identifiable people.
- Free of third-party branding, including legible vessel names and hailing ports.
- Free of landmarks that identify a different place.
- Captioned by subject, never by place, unless the location is confirmed.

Note for stock searches: **"commercial fishing boat" is effectively unusable as a stock
subject here.** Nine Unsplash candidates were screened for this cluster and all five boat
shots carried legible vessel names — the name is painted on the hull, which is intrinsic to
the subject. Stock pineapple fields fail too; the crop is grown in tropical highlands and
every candidate had mountains on the horizon.
