## Removed 2026-08-16 — Honda Classic spectator photos

Eleven files were deleted from the repo on John's instruction. Six lived here as
`pga-national-002` · `003` · `004` · `005` · `007` · `008`; five more were near-duplicates
of the same frames sitting in `public/public/Palm Beach Gardens/PGA National/` as
`IMG_1775` · `1777` · `1778` · `1780` · `1783`.

None were ever committed, so they are not recoverable from git history — the originals live
in John's own archive.

They are recorded here so nobody re-imports them from that archive later. Every one carried
both problems at once:

**Identifiable professional athletes.** Tiger Woods (`002`, `003`, `008`), Rory McIlroy
(`005`, `007`), Rickie Fowler (`004` — his caddie's bib reads FOWLER). These are not
incidental figures in a crowd; they are the subject of the frame and instantly
recognisable.

**Heavy third-party branding.** The Honda Classic, HondaJet, PGA Tour, TaylorMade, Cobra,
Titleist and Tire Kingdom marks appear across the set on tee signage, caddie bibs, staff
credentials and bags.

The concern is not that the photos were taken — John was a paying spectator and taking
them was fine. It is the **use**: a community gallery on a site that sells real estate is
commercial use, and the hero doubles as the page's OG image. Florida has a statutory right
of publicity covering unauthorised commercial use of a person's likeness. Putting Tiger
Woods on a "homes for sale at PGA National" page is squarely the kind of use that statute
contemplates, and it is not something to decide from a photo folder.

**This is a flag, not a legal opinion.** If these images are ever wanted, the question
belongs with the brokerage's counsel first — not with whoever is wiring up a gallery.

## ⚠️ Do not borrow the PGA photos from `../port-st-lucie/`

John shot PGA Golf Club and PGA Village on 2026-08-19 and those ten frames live in
`../port-st-lucie/` as `local-pga-*`. They are **Port St. Lucie**, 50 miles north, and have
nothing to do with PGA National in Palm Beach Gardens. Given that this folder is short of
photography and those files are clean and available, this is the mistake waiting to happen.
The tells: the monument sign reads `1916 PERFECT DRIVE`, the clock tower reads `PGA VILLAGE`.
