## Why these three

The brief was **Old Florida** rather than resort Florida: oak canopy, Spanish moss,
mangrove, still water and sand roads — the landscape of western Palm City and the South
Fork, not palms-and-poolside.

Searching by place name is useless, so these were selected **by subject** and then each
one opened full-size and checked before use.

## Rejected, and why

- **`xGWK9MzfqSQ`** — a gorgeous live oak avenue, rejected because **headstones are
  visible on the right**. It is a cemetery drive, almost certainly Bonaventure in
  Savannah. Wrong on both honesty and tone.
- **`SBfCxmWqcNg`** — "brown horse grazing in a fenced green field." At full size the
  houses on the horizon are unmistakably Northern European and the light is flat and
  grey. Not Florida.
- **`iVINr8-ZFmY`**, **`DWz1CeK4za0`** — main-street shots carrying legible third-party
  business signage, and in one case bare deciduous trees.
- **`NCgzhetqnYc`**, **`mNVYCwQJTiI`** — golf courses that read as Mediterranean or
  resort rather than Old Florida.

## Caveat worth recording

`stock-mangrove-tunnel.jpg` carries a heavier load of branch epiphytes than a typical
Florida mangrove creek — it may well be Central American. It is captioned by subject and
makes no location claim, but a genuine South Fork frame would be better.

## Screening rules applied

- **Free of identifiable people.**
- **Free of third-party branding** and legible signage.
- **Free of landmarks or context that identify a different place** — including
  headstones, non-Florida architecture and non-Florida ecology.
- **Captioned by subject, never by place.**
